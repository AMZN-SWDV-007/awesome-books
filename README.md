### Discover & Track Books

* [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/) - Discover new books through personalized recommendations, reviews, ratings, and reading lists.
* [The StoryGraph](https://app.thestorygraph.com/) - Get personalized recommendations and detailed reading statistics based on your habits.
* [BookBub](https://www.bookbub.com/) - Find curated book recommendations and alerts for free and discounted ebooks.
* [LibraryThing](https://www.librarything.com/) - Organize your personal library and connect with a community of passionate readers.
* [Literary Hub](https://lithub.com/) - Explore literary news, essays, interviews, and book reviews from leading writers and critics.

### Read, Borrow & Buy

* [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/) - Access over 70,000 free public-domain ebooks in multiple formats.
* [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/) - Borrow millions of digital books through the Internet Archive's online library.
* [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/) - Download carefully formatted, high-quality public-domain ebooks for free.
* [Bookshop.org](https://bookshop.org/) - Buy books online while supporting independent bookstores.
* [WorldCat](https://search.worldcat.org/) - Search the collections of thousands of libraries worldwide to find books near you.
